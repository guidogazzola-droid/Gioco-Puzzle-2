# Privacy Policy — Fieldweave

**Last updated: 27 August 2026**

## The short version

Fieldweave does not collect anything about you. There is no account, no
sign-up, no analytics, and no advertising network. The game itself makes no
network request of any kind; the only time an address is fetched is when you
tap an advertisement, and then it is a browser doing it, at your instruction.

That is not a statement of intent, it is a description of how the app is
built — and you can check part of it yourself: switch on Airplane Mode and the
whole game still works.

## What the app stores, and where

Your progress is written to a single file in the app's private storage on your
device: the levels you have finished, your stars, your gems, the styles you own
and have equipped, your settings, and which daily puzzles you have played.

That file never leaves your device. We cannot read it. Deleting the app deletes
it.

## What we receive

Nothing directly.

Apple gives us anonymous, aggregated sales and download reports for the App
Store. They contain nothing that identifies you.

## Apple services the app uses

Two parts of the game are handled by Apple rather than by us.

**Purchases and subscriptions.** Everything bought in Fieldweave goes through
the App Store. We never see your name, your payment method, or your Apple
Account. We only learn, in aggregate, that a purchase happened.

**Game Center (optional).** If you sign in to Game Center, your scores and your
Game Center player name are sent to Apple so that leaderboards can work. This is
Apple's service under Apple's privacy policy; what reaches us is a position on
a board, not your identity. The whole game works if you never sign in —
leaderboards are the only thing you miss.

Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Advertising

The advertisements in Fieldweave are our own. One of them is for VidiVadi
Planner, our trip planner; the other offers this game's Pro subscription. There
is no third-party advertising network, no advertising SDK, and no advertising
identifier anywhere in the app.

Both are drawn from artwork and text inside the app, so showing you one makes
no network request and sends nothing anywhere. Nothing about you is used to
choose which one you see: they simply alternate.

Tapping one opens a website in a browser view inside the game. From that point
the site's own privacy policy applies - www.vidivadi.com has its own - exactly
as if you had typed the address yourself.

If a third-party advertising network is ever added, this page and the app's App
Store privacy label will be updated in the same release that introduces it, and
that network's data practices will be named here.

## Tracking

The app does not track you across other apps or websites and never asks
permission to do so. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected
data types.

## Children

Fieldweave is rated 4+ and suitable for every age. Because it collects no
personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask a company for a copy of the personal
data it holds about you, and ask it to correct or delete that data. We hold
none, so there is nothing for us to send, correct or erase. Everything the game
knows about you is in the save file on your device, and uninstalling the app
removes it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Any change to what
the app collects ships in the same release as the app version that causes it —
never before, never after.
