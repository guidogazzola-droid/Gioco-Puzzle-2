## Common questions

**I bought something and it has not appeared.**
Open Settings inside the game and tap *Restore purchases*. Purchases belong to
the Apple Account that made them, so check you are signed in to the same one.

**I changed device, or reinstalled, and my progress is gone.**
Progress is stored on the device and is not synced to iCloud. Purchases and
subscriptions restore on any device with *Restore purchases*, but levels, stars
and gems do not travel with them.

**How do I cancel Pro?**
Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Line Flow SW Pro › Cancel Subscription.
Cancelling stops the next renewal — Pro stays active until the end of the period
you have already paid for.

**I want a refund.**
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**This level looks impossible.**
It isn't. Every board is built already solved and then taken apart, so an
unsolvable puzzle cannot be generated. If you are stuck, a hint completes one
colour for you — it caps that level at two stars.

**Can I play offline?**
All of it. Only the Game Center leaderboards need a connection.

**The colours are hard to tell apart.**
Settings › *Colour-blind assist* marks every endpoint with its own letter, so
pairs can be matched by shape as well as by hue.

**The animations make me uncomfortable.**
Settings › *Reduce motion* stills the animated backdrops and shortens the
opening screen. The app also follows the system Reduce Motion setting on its
own.
